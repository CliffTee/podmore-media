## 14. Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including legal, tax, accounting, operational and dispute-resolution requirements.

### Enquiries that do not become customers

Normally retained for up to 12 months after the last meaningful communication, unless there is a reason to keep them longer.

### Customer, contract and transaction records

Normally retained for the duration of the customer relationship and for up to six years afterwards where reasonably required for tax, accounting, contractual, complaint or legal purposes.

### Project materials and deliverables

Normally retained while required to provide the service and for a reasonable period afterwards for support, record-keeping and dispute resolution.

We may delete or archive working copies sooner where they are no longer needed.

### Brand Voice Questionnaire and customer form responses

Normally retained while the customer relationship remains active and afterwards where reasonably required for service records, complaints, legal obligations or repeat work.

### Marketing records

Kept while you remain subscribed or while we are otherwise permitted to contact you.

Limited suppression-list information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to ensure that an unsubscribe request continues to be respected.

### Website and security logs

Normally retained for a limited period determined by the hosting or security provider, unless needed to investigate a security incident, fraud or legal issue.

### Complaints and legal claims

Relevant records may be retained until the complaint or claim is resolved and for an appropriate period afterwards.

We may keep information for longer where required by law, needed for an active dispute, necessary to prevent fraud, or you ask us to retain it and we have a lawful basis to do so.
