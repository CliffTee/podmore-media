# Cookie Policy

**Effective date:** 5 July 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Podmore Media uses cookies and similar technologies on this website.

## 1. What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies are small text files placed on a device. Similar technologies include scripts, tags, pixels and browser storage.

Some technologies are necessary for a website to work or to remember a visitor's privacy choices. Optional analytics and marketing technologies require consent and remain off unless the visitor chooses to allow them.

## 2. Consent categories

### Necessary

Necessary technologies support essential website functions and remember cookie preferences. They cannot be switched off through the preferences panel.

### Analytics

Analytics is optional and off by default. If a visitor accepts Analytics, Google Analytics 4 helps Podmore Media understand page visits and selected website interactions.

### Marketing

Marketing is optional and off by default. If a visitor accepts Marketing, Meta Pixel helps Podmore Media measure visits and selected actions for advertising measurement, audience creation and retargeting.

## 3. Technologies used

### pm_cookie_consent

- **Provider:** Podmore Media
- **Category:** Necessary
- **Purpose:** Records the consent version, the visitor's Analytics and Marketing choices, and the date the choices were saved.
- **Duration:** Six months

### _ga

- **Provider:** Google Analytics 4
- **Category:** Analytics
- **Purpose:** Helps distinguish website visitors for analytics reporting.
- **Duration:** Up to two years
- **When used:** Only after Analytics consent.

### _ga_61HX1GVR89

- **Provider:** Google Analytics 4
- **Category:** Analytics
- **Purpose:** Maintains session information for the Podmore Media GA4 property.
- **Duration:** Up to two years
- **When used:** Only after Analytics consent.

### _fbp

- **Provider:** Meta Platforms Ireland Limited
- **Category:** Marketing
- **Purpose:** Identifies a browser for advertising delivery, advertising measurement, audience creation, retargeting and site analytics services.
- **Duration:** 90 days
- **When used:** Only after Marketing consent.

### _fbc

- **Provider:** Meta Platforms Ireland Limited
- **Category:** Marketing
- **Purpose:** Stores Meta click information when a visitor arrives through a Meta advertising link, supporting advertising measurement, audience creation and retargeting.
- **Duration:** 90 days
- **When used:** Only after Marketing consent and when relevant Meta click information is present.

Cookie durations are maximum durations. Cookies may be removed earlier, including when a visitor withdraws consent or clears browser data.

## 4. Google Analytics 4

Podmore Media uses Google Analytics 4 with Measurement ID G-61HX1GVR89 only after Analytics consent.

GA4 may process information such as the page visited, page title, referring page, device and browser information, approximate location, visit timing, engagement information and configured events. On the ebook sales page, configured events include the start of a Stripe checkout after a purchase button is selected.

GA4 is configured so that Google Signals, advertising features and ad personalisation are disabled. Podmore Media does not send Stripe session identifiers to GA4 and does not treat a purchase-button click as a completed purchase.

## 5. Meta Pixel

Podmore Media uses Meta Pixel with Pixel ID **1512324313721969** only after Marketing consent. The Pixel is provided by Meta Platforms Ireland Limited.

Meta Pixel may process information such as the page visited, browser and device information, IP address, referring information, Meta cookie identifiers and configured actions. Podmore Media uses this information for advertising measurement, audience creation and retargeting.

On the ebook sales page, selecting a purchase button sends an **InitiateCheckout** event after Marketing consent. The event identifies the ebook, price, currency and button position. It is not a completed Purchase event.

Meta Pixel is configured without a noscript tracking image. Its script, initialisation and events do not run before Marketing consent. Query strings are removed before the Pixel loads, and page URLs sent by Podmore Media exclude query strings so that Stripe session identifiers are not sent to Meta.

Meta's current Cookies Policy documents a 90-day lifespan for **_fbp** and **_fbc**. Meta may update its technologies and documentation over time.

## 6. International processing

Meta Platforms Ireland Limited and other Meta group companies or service providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where UK data-protection law requires safeguards, the relevant Meta data-processing and transfer terms, adequacy regulations, the UK Addendum to approved contractual clauses, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or another lawful transfer mechanism may apply.

## 7. Changing or withdrawing consent

Visitors can select “Cookie settings” in the footer on any page to reopen the preferences panel.

Withdrawing Analytics consent prevents further GA4 activity, removes accessible GA cookies and reloads the page without loading GA4.

Withdrawing Marketing consent prevents further Podmore Media Meta Pixel tracking, removes accessible **_fbp** and **_fbc** cookies where possible, and reloads the page without loading Meta Pixel.

Rejecting optional cookies does not prevent access to the website or its ordinary functions.

## 8. Third-party websites

The website links to services including Stripe, Google Forms, AWeber and Brevo for purchases, forms or service delivery. When a visitor follows a link or submits a form to another provider, that provider controls its own website and may use technologies under its own privacy and cookie information.
